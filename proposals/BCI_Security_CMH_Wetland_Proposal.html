Proposal: Supply of 15 x Chery Tiggo Cross 1.5 LiT CVT for the BCI Security fleet

Dear [Name],

CMH Wetland is reaching out to propose a Chery fleet for BCI Security. A security operation runs on vehicles that start every morning, cover long daily distances, cost little to run, and go back on the road quickly when they need a service. This proposal introduces a vehicle that meets that brief, a fleet price for 15 units, how you can own them, and the warranty and aftersales that keep the fleet moving. It is an unsolicited approach; there is no obligation, and we would value the chance to present it to your team.

1. The opportunity

A fleet of 15 vehicles would cover BCI Security's patrol, response and supervisory work. The priorities for that kind of fleet are reliability on high daily mileage, low running cost per kilometre, enough space for officers and equipment, and a vehicle that looks the part on site. The Chery Tiggo Cross meets all four.

  • High daily mileage on patrol and response routes.
  • Reliable starts and all-day duty cycles.
  • Low fuel and maintenance cost across 15 vehicles.
  • Presence and ground clearance for mixed-condition access roads.

2. Recommended vehicle: Chery Tiggo Cross 1.5 LiT CVT

The Tiggo Cross is Chery's compact SUV. The 1.5 LiT CVT pairs an efficient naturally aspirated petrol engine with a smooth automatic gearbox and an unusually generous equipment list for the price. It is a practical, low-fatigue vehicle for officers who spend a full shift behind the wheel.

  • Presence. SUV stance and height suit a security brand and handle rough access roads.
  • Running cost. A claimed 7.3 L/100 km and an auto gearbox cut fuel use and driver fatigue.
  • Comfort and safety. A 10.25 inch dual screen, cruise control, reverse camera and four airbags.
  • Peace of mind. A 5 year / 150 000 km vehicle warranty and 10 year / 1 million km engine warranty.

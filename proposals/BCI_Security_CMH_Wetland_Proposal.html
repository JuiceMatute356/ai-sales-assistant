3. Recommended Vehicle — Chery Tiggo Cross 1.5 LiT CVT

The Tiggo Cross is Chery's compact SUV, and the 1.5 LiT CVT derivative pairs an efficient naturally aspirated petrol engine with a smooth automatic transmission and an unusually generous equipment list for the price — a practical, low-fatigue vehicle for officers who spend their shift behind the wheel.

Why it suits a security fleet

  • Presence. SUV stance and height project authority and shrug off rough access roads and kerbs.
  • Running cost. A frugal 7.3 L/100 km claimed figure and an auto gearbox cut fuel burn and driver fatigue on long shifts.
  • Comfort & safety. A 10.25″ dual-screen cabin, cruise control, reverse camera and four airbags keep officers alert, connected and protected.
  • Ownership peace of mind. A 5-year/150 000 km vehicle warranty and 10-year/1 million km engine warranty cap downside risk across the fleet.

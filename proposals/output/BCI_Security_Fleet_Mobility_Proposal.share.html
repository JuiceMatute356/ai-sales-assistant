1. The opportunity

CMH Westrand values its existing business relationship with BCI Security. We would welcome the opportunity to extend that relationship into fleet mobility by developing a vehicle package aligned with BCI's operational requirements, budget and replacement plans.

Our objective is a practical, commercially structured solution that considers the total cost of ownership, vehicle suitability and ongoing dealership support — not simply a vehicle price. Where this proposal refers to cost reduction, it is an objective to be evaluated against BCI's current fleet once the relevant figures are available, not a saving we are claiming in advance.

4. Fleet partnership and support

A fleet works best when the dealership relationship is set up for it. The following are support capabilities CMH Westrand proposes to put in place for BCI.

Dedicated contact
A direct point of contact for vehicle enquiries and coordination.
Fleet quotations
Structured quotations based on the required number of vehicles and specifications.
Finance assistance
Assistance exploring suitable finance structures, subject to affordability, credit approval and lender terms.
Service coordination
Assistance coordinating servicing and workshop bookings, subject to dealer capacity and agreed arrangements.
Accessories & branding
Optional accessories and vehicle branding, subject to quotation and approval.
Vehicle delivery
Delivery coordination, subject to stock availability and agreed timelines.
Future fleet planning
An opportunity to discuss phased purchases and future replacement requirements.
What this does and does not promise
These are proposed support capabilities, offered in good faith — not contractual guarantees. Priority workshop treatment, courtesy or replacement vehicles, guaranteed uptime, special service rates and dedicated fleet-management resources are not implied by this document. Each can be explored and, if agreed, confirmed in writing.

6. Next steps

We would welcome the opportunity to understand BCI Security's fleet requirements and develop a commercial proposal aligned with your business. A short discussion would let us confirm your vehicle requirements, replacement plans, preferred acquisition structure and budget — after which CMH Westrand can prepare a detailed quotation for your consideration.

To prepare an accurate quotation, we would need:

  1. Current fleet size
  2. Number of vehicles required
  3. Current vehicle makes and models
  4. Expected replacement timeline
  5. Intended vehicle use and operating environment
  6. Preferred purchasing or finance structure
  7. Target budget per vehicle

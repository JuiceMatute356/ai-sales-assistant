Ownership, your way

Three potential ownership structures.

The fleet can be structured around BCI's cash flow and how you prefer to hold your assets. Firm figures follow once the preferred route and requirements are confirmed.

Cash purchase
Outright ownership from day one, with no vehicle finance interest cost. Suitable where BCI prefers to acquire and hold the vehicles directly.
Instalment sale
Spread the vehicle cost over an agreed finance term, subject to credit approval and applicable finance terms. Ownership transfers to BCI in accordance with the finance agreement.
Full-maintenance lease
A structured monthly payment that can incorporate agreed maintenance and operating costs, subject to the selected lease provider and contract terms. This can provide greater predictability for fleet budgeting.

No pricing appears in this proposal by design. We prepare transparent figures around the ownership route BCI prefers, once that route is agreed.

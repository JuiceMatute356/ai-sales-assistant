Ownership, your way

Three ways onto the road.

The fleet can be structured around your cash flow and how BCI prefers to hold its assets. We will put firm figures to whichever route suits you the moment you want them.

Cash purchase
Outright ownership from day one, no finance cost. The simplest route for assets you intend to keep for the long haul.
Instalment sale
Spread the fleet over a fixed term with a deposit, with ownership passing to BCI at the end. Subject to credit approval.
Full-maintenance lease
One fixed monthly figure with servicing, maintenance and tyres included. Easiest to budget, and it keeps the fleet fresh.
